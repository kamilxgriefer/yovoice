## What was NOT verified — read this before believing anything ships

- **No device, no simulator, no real browser — anywhere in this build.**
  Every visual claim rests on frames rendered by the Flutter test renderer
  under `yovoice-evidence/2026-09-25/…` and `…/2026-09-19/next-build/…`. The
  countdown and haptics, the friend-request surfaces, the full-bleed banner
  (frame cost on a low-end Android and on web CanvasKit, the status bar on a
  physical iPhone, how old banners cropped for the 23% strip look), the
  podcast queue and the re-mint, the Bug button's placement and its capture
  over video views, and the whole next-build half are UNVERIFIED on
  hardware.
- **Nothing is deployed.** Every backend defect fixed here is still live for
  every user: a one-minute voice note still fails on released builds, the
  push boundary, the reminders, the comment notifications, the server media
  and reactions, the takeover remediation and the bug reports do not exist in
  production yet.
- **Provider behaviour is unproven.** The LiveKit webhook has never had a
  delivery read back, so voice-time accounting, the provider-driven end of an
  empty channel and the lowering of a departed listener's hand are unproven;
  the provider drill is not done; the stale LIVE badges already in
  production are untouched (the repair script has never run there).
- **Measurements that do not exist yet:** the real per-platform overage of a
  capped take (ADR-217), how long a promoted listener waits for a new token
  (the client retries for about 30 s), the takeover sweeper's production
  throughput, and production's revocation behaviour for a pre-linked Google
  identity (ADR-222's canary).
- **Nothing was read from production** in this documentation pass, and no
  console was touched by it. The production facts above (245 functions, the
  2026-09-19 and 2026-09-14 rulesets, the web serving 3.0.0+35) come from the
  earlier release records and are to be re-read in step 0 of the deploy.
- **Off by design:** GIPHY, the bug-report alert channels, takeover Phase 2,
  App Check enforcement, and the thirteen held functions.

## Release notes — YO Voice 3.1.0 (36)

User-facing, compared with 3.0.0 (34), which is what testers have. **No links
and no domain names**, because the tester e-mails carry none. **Do not send
either version until build 36 is deployed and installed**: several lines are
backend changes (voice notes, notifications, reminders, reactions and media,
the LIVE badge, raised hands, bug reports) and are false until the deploy is
read back; the LIVE-badge line also needs the LiveKit webhook confirmed.

### English

> **What's new in YO Voice 3.1.0**
>
> **New sounds.** Warm, soft wooden tones for joining and leaving
> conversations, the microphone, notifications and calls. A call sounds
> different from a message from the very first moment.
>
> **Scrub through a Yeel.** Drag along the timeline to move through a Yeel,
> or use the arrow keys. The Voice story player takes a finger too.
>
> **Call a friend from their profile.** Call, Video, Message and More sit
> right under the bio. From More you can send a voice message, invite them to
> one of your servers, or report them.
>
> **See it before you send it.** Photos and videos you pick from your
> library, and files you add to a Company server, show you exactly what you
> chose — with its size and length — before anything uploads.
>
> **Delete or leave a server from the list.** It is on the server row itself
> and in the server's settings. Deleting asks you to type the server's name
> first.
>
> **Emoji, reactions, photos and videos in server channels.** Tap and hold a
> message to react, announcements included. Send photos and videos of up to a
> minute, and delete your own messages.
>
> **New notifications.** Comments on your Voice Moments and Yeels, mentions
> in those comments, server event reminders, and a promotion or ownership
> transfer in a server now reach you. Turn them on or off under "Moments &
> Yeels" and "Servers" in your notification settings.
>
> **The LIVE badge tells the truth.** A voice channel stops showing LIVE
> about a minute after the last person leaves.
>
> **A one-minute voice message sends.** A voice message that runs to the
> full minute used to fail; now it goes through. In the last ten seconds you
> see a countdown, and when the recording stops by itself it stays ready to
> send.
>
> **Friend requests ask first.** Every friend request shows two clear
> buttons, Accept and Decline — in the notifications, on the banner, on the
> person's profile and in your Friends list. Nothing accepts a request by
> accident, and adding someone who has already asked you shows you their
> request instead.
>
> **Your photo fills the whole top of your profile.** Your background photo
> now spans the full header, behind your picture and your name, and fades
> softly into the page.
>
> **Podcasts: hosts see who wants to speak.** When a listener asks to speak,
> the host sees the request right away and can approve or decline it. When
> someone is brought on stage or moved back to the audience, they stay
> connected. Hosts also see a small dot when listeners have asked new
> questions.
>
> **Report a bug from the app.** "Report a bug" is in Settings and in More.
> While we are testing, a small Bug button floats on the screen of the mobile
> app: drag it where it suits you, or hide it in Settings. Add a screenshot if you like —
> you see it before it is sent.
>
> **Safer sign-in.** If an account was ever registered with your e-mail
> address and a password you never confirmed, signing in with Google or Apple
> now secures it and ends every other session. You may be asked to sign in
> once more.
>
> **And more.** GIFs are coming. Premium now describes Servers as they are
> today. Better screen-reader labels and focus on the new buttons, and many
> small fixes.
