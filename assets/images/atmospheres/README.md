# YO destination atmospheres

Original generated environment artwork for the approved live-first Home and
the five normal navigation destinations. These are decorative bundled assets,
not user photos, actual rooms, or evidence of activity. Generated with the
built-in image-generation tool on 2026-09-05, then encoded as metadata-free
WebP at quality 78. The five images total less than 300 KB. No remote request,
preloading of all scenes, animated media or runtime blur is required.

| Destination | File | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | home-lounge.webp | Welcome lounge with evening city window and armchairs |
| Rooms | rooms-lounge.webp | Approved plum sofa, microphones and YO neon lounge |
| Chats | chats-lounge.webp | Quiet two-armchair conversation corner |
| Moments | moments-studio.webp | Creator microphone and listening studio |
| More / Settings | more-study.webp | Calm architectural study and lit niche |

`YoPageBackground` chooses exactly one scene, behind content. It keeps the
normal semantic canvas, uses bounded image alpha and decode width, and disables
decorative imagery in high-contrast mode. Uploaded room covers always win over
the lounge fallback. Immersive calls, recording, media viewers and auth remain
unchanged. Original PNGs remain in the user's Codex generated-images directory.

## Prompt set

Shared prompt: ONE portrait 9:19.5 high-end photographic environmental
background for YO Voice mobile app, not a screenshot. No interface, icons,
buttons, headings, status bar, phone frame, people or watermarks. Reference the
official YO logo silhouette as neon wall art and the approved Home lounge
photo for materials. Photoreal premium architectural interior, graphite walls,
tactile fabrics, smoked glass, restrained violet/magenta practical light and
warm amber accents. Quiet dark upper 30%, furniture in middle/lower half,
vignette edges and near-black bottom. No other logos or written text.

Room variation: plush plum sofa, low dark round coffee table, amber candle,
two podcast boom microphones, nighttime city window, houseplant, exact YO
neon above sofa on right. Inviting conversation space, wide interior framing.

Home variation: entrance/social lounge with floor-to-ceiling evening city
window, two curved armchairs, coffee table, violet architectural niche, small
YO neon on right wall, leafy plant, charcoal upholstery; no large sofa.

Chats variation: intimate listening nook, two facing charcoal armchairs,
smoked-glass side table, amber lamp, violet indirect light and acoustic wall
detail, small YO neon; extra quiet negative space, no chat/phone symbols.

Moments variation: creator studio with black condenser microphone and pop
filter at right, headphones on lower-left console, violet acoustic panels,
YO neon, warm practical light; no screens, text or busy control panels.

More variation: minimal study, curved plaster niche, lilac lighting, lounge
chair and low table, books without readable text, frosted-glass YO emblem;
most understated scene, warm graphite shadows, no screens or gadgets.
